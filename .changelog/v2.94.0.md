# Release v2.94.0 - Lip-Sync Firefly

Released: 2026-10-12

## Highlights

- **Local performance shots:** Music videos can lip-sync to the song with local LTX models, with stronger audio alignment and supporting-cast references.
- **Shot-by-shot control:** Make and lock each still and clip, choose a renderer per shot, and split shots at a chosen moment.
- **Smoother previews:** Watch actual footage in the storyboard, step through exact frames, save a frame, and preview the film look during playback.
- **Clearer video costs:** Paid clip estimates account for billed minimum durations, show spending by model, and reserve a separate renderer for end-frame shots.
- **Reliable personal history:** Signal sync includes committed messages still awaiting checkpoint, and Timeline totals cover the entire day.
- **Friendlier small screens:** Prompt Manager prioritizes the selected editor, while music-video controls leave more room for the preview.

## Added

- Local LTX lip-sync performance shots, audio-driven planning, and a local renderer choice for individual performance shots.
- Separate still and video locks that protect finished shots from replacement or structural edits until unlocked.
- Per-shot rendering controls, timeline-ordered shot insertion, and splitting at a chosen time while retaining still approvals.
- Frame stepping, frame saving, and previous/next shot navigation in music-video previews.
- Opening title cards with additional fonts and a classic music-television layout, configured alongside the project's look.
- Supporting-cast photographic sheets and shot references for recurring characters.
- Style-break end frames, continuity from the preceding shot's final cut frame, and Grok clip options up to 15 seconds.
- Mood-board poster previews and a collage layout that leads with the poster.

## Changed

- Music-video planning and production are organized into Plan and Shots, with still locking beside the player.
- Locked stills survive cut moves, lyric retiming, and wording changes that preserve their approved visual content.
- Silent video models receive physical motion instructions without lyric, beat, or film-look cues that they cannot interpret as audio.
- Paid video quotes reflect billed rates and minimum clip lengths; project settings break estimates down by model.
- Autopilot accepts direction during a run, preserves rendered clips through a backup cut, and retries infrastructure failures within its existing limits.
- Local Extend renders preserve source frames and avoid repeated audio encoding.
- Film looks apply to footage, excerpts, and storyboard playback, with lighter effects while previews are moving.
- Prompt Manager and orchestration profiles distinguish unavailable reads from empty inventories and offer recovery controls.

## Fixed

- Signal sync reads a consistent committed snapshot, authenticates every page, preserves message row identities, and leaves its cursor unchanged on invalid snapshots.
- Timeline day totals and hourly counts include the full day even when the displayed event list is bounded.
- Browser message and activity identities remain stable across sync, cache replay, and upgrades.
- Generated previews contain navigation and network access, and retire stale media connections.
- Agent-backed image generation requires operator authority, while Persistent Mind reports honor current app grants, including cached-report polling.
- Lip-sync takes align with the song when providers pad clips, and local refinement keeps the conditioning audio fixed.
- Storyboard footage starts without a black flash; paused seeks and exact frame steps survive preview loading.
- Aspect-ratio cropping is consistent for generated stills and extracted clip frames.
- Locked storyboard inputs and compositions survive rejected automated plans, and generated candidates remain available after partial failures.
- Opening title cards preserve entered text and support bundled fonts in composition renders.
- Portrait and square Code Animation previews retain their proportions instead of being stretched by independent size limits.
- Film-look and title-card edits made while a preview opens are applied once it is ready.
- Preview startup pipe failures fail the request safely, and failed or cancelled initialization closes its owned browser before removing temporary assets.
- Shot edits, structural changes, and take selection recheck video locks within the same write transaction, including rejection of a selected take.
- Local rendering can recover once from a Metal watchdog termination using smaller command buffers within one shared relaunch budget.
- TUI agents continue waiting while the model visibly thinks instead of treating a quiet spinner as a completed answer.
- Managed-app source views refresh after updates, and empty orchestration-profile screens show one create action.

## Internal

- Improved test-process exit diagnostics, Windows temporary-file handling, browser-test isolation, and asynchronous fixture completion.
- Updated happy-dom, Vite, and the Node setup action.
- Release documentation now reflects that publication keeps the release branch pinned to its verified tree.
