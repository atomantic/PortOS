# Release v2.113.0 - Storyboard Squirrel

Released: 2026-11-07

## Highlights

- Music video shots now use editable shot details, real-world subject sizes, and connected route maps to keep scenes consistent.
- Render several clips for a shot at once, with clearer controls and reference support across more video models.
- Explore native coding-app usage by account and machine, with clearer coverage and faster reads of large histories.
- Phone layouts, touch targets, and text fields work better across the dashboard, Brain, Chief of Staff, and creative tools.
- Concurrent saves and interrupted connections preserve more of your work and recover without leaving stale spinners.

## Added

- Shot details can be edited in the shot drawer and used to build still and motion prompts.
- Casting places and objects can record real-world size, while route maps connect places and describe landmarks and viewing direction.
- Native usage views show provider coverage, account selection, and totals from configured peers without sharing raw transcripts.
- Native usage migration tools support isolated recovery rehearsals and an operator-controlled cutover.
- Local Branches can delete individual merged branches.

## Changed

- Music video shot-production tools load when the Shots step opens, reducing the initial page bundle.
- Shot editing brings prompts, settings, and rendering into one drawer, with growing text fields and clearer reference guidance.
- Runway models that support image references receive them, and video cost estimates cover the available model rates.
- Music video clips use their selected references and supported camera guides, with song audio omitted for models that cannot accept it.
- Goal progress, check-ins, and practice sessions follow your local calendar day.
- Ambiguous WhatsApp imports require an explicit day/month order.
- AI assignment cards and dense controls fit narrower screens and offer larger touch targets.

## Fixed

- Music video and Writers Room views recover render state after reconnecting, and failed cast-guide reads offer Retry before rendering.
- A final music video render cannot complete a project after its song has changed.
- Codex image jobs retain the image belonging to their prompt, while large native transcript histories are read more efficiently.
- Usage reporting preserves image renders, unknown counters, incomplete-history warnings, and deduplicated retained records.
- Overlapping Ask, draft, genome, Digital Twin, and subscription updates preserve concurrent edits and restoration records.
- Unreadable sync state and iMessage privacy settings stop unsafe updates instead of silently resetting saved state.
- GitHub notification assistance preserves prior hand-offs through repeated triage.
- Host-control checks cover additional creative actions and preview sockets, and peer access respects shared record categories.
- Release synchronization can retire its own locked checkout and explain other cleanup holds.
