# Release v2.115.0 - Lockstep Lynx

Released: 2026-11-09

## Highlights

- Local media runtimes (image, video, music) now install from hash-pinned lock files on Apple Silicon, Linux and Windows, so installs and repairs reproduce the same verified packages.
- Music Video shots get a single editable prompt, per-shot clip length and video model, and an optional "LTX 2.5 References" project render mode.
- The Previz step grows up: figures crouch, sit, walk and run; sets are lit by the story clock; missing greyboxes are drafted from shots; and build/sync work shows up as live server jobs.
- Music Video singers and lyric timing are more accurate: the singer is carried per lyric line, held "ooh/ah" lines are re-timed from the vocal stem, and the director can split, merge, move and rename song sections.
- Weekly, biweekly and monthly scheduled jobs keep their chosen weekday however late a run lands, and loop intervals can no longer fire every millisecond.
- Managed model hosts recover on their own: a stalled vLLM runtime is detected and restarted, and a client hang-up no longer crashes the host.

## Added

- **Music Video shots:** one editable prompt per shot, per-shot source clip length and video model, an "End clip here" trim action, Hide and Delete in the media preview, and overnight local clip renders queued for chosen shots.
- **LTX 2.5 References:** a project-wide render mode, an adapter download shown in render settings, and production-run clips that honor it.
- **Previz:** named props, lighting by the story clock, crouching/sitting/walking/running figures, selfie cuts showing the camera holder, drafting missing greyboxes from shots, a generic travel model and per-road street infrastructure, and Previz work shown as server jobs with live progress.
- Frame checks can run in place, use a per-project check model, and verify each character's always-visible details; a "Tighten prompts" action trims shot prompts that repeat themselves.
- Pi is the default harness for model host clients, and a stalled vLLM runtime on the model host is detected and restarted.
- Scheduled dependency updates now audit and bump Python pins and locks.
- The unreleased-peers quality warning names the unavailable peers and links to their Instances card.
- PortOS provisions the claim worktree itself for a pinned issue claim.

## Changed

- Local runtimes for MLX and CUDA stacks (mflux, MusicGen, MiniMax, ACE-Step, AudioLDM2, MuScriptor, FLUX.2/FLUX.3, FastVideo, Wan 2.2, LTX-2.5) install from hash-pinned locks; Image Gen and Video Gen repair stale environments from those locks.
- The shared mflux runtime moves to mflux 0.24, mlx 0.32.4 and Pillow 13.
- Music Video AI planners draft one shot prompt instead of separate frame and motion text, keep it short with one action, and rewrite rather than append.
- Grok shot clips render at 720p, can use their start frame for layout only, and Gemini clip renders retry when Gemini is at capacity and stay in video mode.
- The World map fits whole sets, uses screen-sized labels and avoids overlaps.
- Music Video take cards lead with their review actions; copy chips stay a 44px touch target on phones.
- Managed app "Update App" restarts only running processes; Run on App lists are alphabetized.

## Fixed

- Weekly, biweekly and monthly jobs keep their scheduled day even when a run lands late, and a picked weekday survives schedule edits.
- Loop intervals cap at the 24-day timer limit, and stopping or deleting a loop stops its in-flight AI run.
- Held "ooh/ah" lyric lines are re-timed from the vocal stem instead of being squeezed.
- Shot card fields save on blur only when edited and refresh from edits made elsewhere; Auto shot references only add set plates a shot names; catalog images of environments and objects can be picked for shots.
- Cast & Sets sheet saves are serialized so an edit right after approval cannot fail; catalog-linked videos can edit their kept direction text.
- Previz people and held cameras stand on sloped ground, riding is shown when the action describes it, and legacy `/media/music-video` links redirect.
- Local video renders fail fast naming truncated text-encoder shards and avoid Metal watchdog aborts from LTX connector weights.
- RSS: non-feed URLs are rejected, RSS 1.0 parses, and unparseable pub dates no longer drop new posts.
- Contacts sharing a phone or email stay separate; Life Calendar activities use stable ids and its controls are touch-sized.
- Touch-target shrinks on tablets and short/narrow windows apply only for fine pointers.
- A stalled owned git child on POSIX settles after a timeout; claim branches are freed once their delivery is retired.

## Internal

- Branch-reconcile, quality-snapshot, claim dispatch and test-stability fixes; the Music Video demo cast was replaced with generic names.
