# Release v2.77.0 - Sonic Sketchbook

Released: 2026-09-25

## Highlights

- **Music Designer:** Create music with AI-written Strudel or Tone.js code, or draw wave sketches that stay with a track and can be remixed later.
- **Model comparisons:** Compare a broader set of sourced public benchmarks, reasoning-effort curves, and measured task costs to find lower-cost near matches.
- **Lighter browsing:** Inbox bodies and media details load when opened, while YouTube ingest history and the Writers Room library load in stable pages.
- **More dependable federation:** Paired machines use scoped credentials and explicit consent for record pushes; sync and Brain relays recover from stalls, version gaps, and restarts.
- **Storage at a glance:** The Data Manager maps disk usage in an interactive treemap, and database backups can be retained or deleted.
- **Smoother creative work:** Code Animation can draft fuller short-film plans, deck prompt writing streams progress, and Chief of Staff settings save as you edit.
- **Safer viewing and clearer status:** Synced email blocks remote images and styling until allowed, screen readers hear when the Chief of Staff timeline is loading, and issue actions fit narrow layouts.

## Added

- The Music Designer can write and play Strudel and Tone.js code in an isolated browser frame, record it as a take, and save drawn wave sketches on tracks for later editing and remixing.
- Code Animation's brief writer can develop a short-film direction with a clear arc, varied beats, visual acting, and a payoff.
- The Data Manager presents storage categories as a disk-usage treemap with category details and cleanup actions.
- Database backup snapshots support retention settings and deletion.
- Deck prompt generation streams progress and displays completed prompts as they are saved.
- Image and video generation can use saved local model defaults, and Chief of Staff configuration saves selections as they change.

## Changed

- Models → Comparison again shows a broad, sourced catalog of benchmark and pricing observations, with effort curves, matched-effort comparisons, and value guidance based on measured task costs. Estimates are labeled and do not drive recommendations.
- Media queues and history use compact views of active or visible jobs; inbox message bodies load on demand. YouTube ingest history and the Writers Room library load stable pages instead of sending and rendering the full history at once.
- Chief of Staff settings are editable immediately, save toggles and selections as you change them, and save number fields when you leave them.
- Issue actions use a compact, wrapping layout on smaller screens, and the Models section keeps its navigation beside the page content.
- Calendar events appear on every day they span.

## Fixed

- Paired peers authenticate with a shared pair secret and receive access only to federation routes. Record pushes require local consent, and peer announcements cannot redirect a stored peer from a different source address.
- Brain records accepted locally or through snapshot reconciliation remain in the outgoing sync log, including after a write failure, crash, or restart. Catalog sync also preserves newer tombstones and mixed-version attachments.
- Federation sync can recover from stalled response bodies, and paired-peer relay sockets remain connected while subscribing to the permitted Chief of Staff events.
- Host commands require an operator session, even through the development proxy; a guest join link can no longer grant a host role.
- Remote images and styles in synced email stay blocked until the reader opts in, and that choice resets when the message changes.
- Chief of Staff recovers unfinished turns after a restart when automatic startup is disabled, and its configuration panel no longer gets stuck in a render loop.
- Media history preserves running cancellation state, and abandoned video renders are cancelled after repeated status failures or a deadline.
- Shutdown rejects late WebSocket upgrades with a complete response, while closed sockets are left untouched.
- Stale integration loads are discarded, Jira credentials survive instance management, and catalog read failures are shown separately from empty results.
- Invalid series identifiers cannot write synced review or outline files outside their series directory.
