# Release v2.87.0 - Surefooted Songbird

Released: 2026-10-05

## Highlights

- **A clearer Music Video journey:** each stage explains what it needs, keeps its own approvals, and links directly to the work that remains.
- **Faster project browsing:** Music Video opens individual projects directly and pages compact summaries, while Tracks loads only the MIDI information it needs.
- **More resilient production:** scene failures remain visible after reload, running jobs reconnect, and autonomous runs wait for the final render before reporting completion.
- **Safer maintenance and backups:** maintenance can drain active work before admitting new jobs, and more creative workflows keep their files and records together across backup snapshots.
- **More expressive code-rendered films:** eight curated film styles guide launch videos, Code Animation, and Music Video compositions, with measured soundtrack loudness.
- **Portable making-of packages:** export selected planning artifacts, storyboards, cast sheets, and provenance as a reviewed ZIP.

## Added

### Music Video

- Stage checklists list every readiness problem with a link to the relevant control; stale approvals explain what changed and can restore retained approved text.
- Scene attention badges, Board filters, keyboard navigation, and a Produce shot strip make missing footage and pending decisions easier to find.
- Frame and clip batches show counts and estimated fal spend before submission, track progress, support partial batches, and let you cancel remaining work.
- Lyric alignment runs as a cancellable job with progress, duplicate-request protection, and reconnection after reload.
- Open review feedback can drive revisions to art direction, storyboard shots, and supported compositions.
- Scene and drawer links, command-palette project search, and voice navigation open the selected work directly.
- Making-of exports include explicitly selected artifact versions, an inventory, relative graphics, timing sheets, provenance, rights declarations, and checksums; missing or unsafe content stays visibly unresolved.
- Explicit shot pools and song conditioning support federated rendering while preserving exact source-audio requirements and uncertain-job ownership.
- Footage projects can prepare vertical social cuts, and the posting panel lets you choose the cut for each supported platform.

### Creative tools and operations

- Code Animation, launch videos, and Music Video code compositions can use curated risograph, blueprint, silent-film, pixel, paper-cut, low-poly, presentation, and line-drawing styles.
- Synthesized voice-over can be transcribed back against its script; manuscript narration offers per-sentence re-rendering and acceptance of what was spoken.
- Launch-video and Code Animation soundtracks can be mastered to -14 LUFS with measured loudness shown in the result; Music Video's exact song master stays unchanged.
- Settings offers a durable maintenance hold that drains owned work, reports blockers, and survives restart without forcing cancellation or silently enabling queued work.
- Instances offers explicitly scoped peer-administration planning and previews; remote administration execution remains unavailable.
- Model storage inventories additional model stores and shared Hugging Face blobs, with bounded delete and cache-clear actions.

## Changed

- Music Video project lists sort by recent activity, group versions, filter by name, and update run state live.
- Setup owns project options, render style, media mode, and production strategy; Song & lyrics follows four numbered steps and folds advanced audio tools away.
- A single Look references panel gathers visual references, and treatment generation uses consistent model routing.
- Art, storyboard, and animated-proof approvals live on Cast & Sets, Board, and Compose respectively; the proof follows composition work.
- Contact sheets are reachable from Board and Produce, filter pending decisions, preview clips on focus or hover, and support frame regeneration.
- Code reviewer settings track failure limits and fallback tiers, while remote API reviews receive their own timeout budget.
- Federated media-model settings offer Enable all and Disable all, with loading feedback.

## Fixed

- Final-render failures no longer leave autonomous Music Video runs marked Finished, and interrupted production reconnects to existing jobs.
- Stale renders and publishing kits are clearly flagged; Publish completes only after every enabled platform has a recorded post.
- Filled publishing tabs survive the former 30-minute limit and can be restored after reload; stale responses cannot overwrite a newly selected project.
- Replacing a song, replanning an existing board, discarding drafts, and deleting excerpts use explicit safeguards; a registered proof cannot be deleted.
- Music Video previews follow the current composition and avoid reloading for unrelated saves; stage indicators avoid false incomplete flicker.
- Owned composition browsers finish teardown on cancellation, and renderer settlement retains uncertain cleanup as a maintenance blocker.
- More Universe Builder, Music Video, Code Animation, Writers Room, voice, music, sprite, LoRA, mood-board, attachment, mesh, and peer-import workflows preserve file-and-record consistency during backups. Backup manifests report the covered scope; this is not a claim of global consistency for every asset owner.
- Calendar sync preserves events outside its fetched window, Spotify imports deduplicate consistently, and RSS batches deduplicate and retain items by publication date.
- Attachment sweeps and media reconciliation preserve concurrently downloaded or indexed assets, and failed mood-board imports preserve shared posters.
- Peer queries stay within the documented federation surface, and certificate provisioning requires operator authority.
- Agent transcripts no longer persist raw terminal control sequences on empty completion signals; Antigravity usage includes conversations absent from its history index.
- Voice workers recover from spawn and stream errors, command completion runs once, and repository-pull timers are cleared after settlement.
- MortalLoom serializes overlapping store writes, and claim reconciliation preserves live ownership during branch binding.
- Repository source counts refresh after git operations, agent cards show full Claude model versions, and loading states and compact layouts are clearer.

## Internal

- Updated Google Calendar, Lucide, Vite, and bundled slashdo dependencies.
- Improved regression coverage and test isolation for media rendering, scene events, browser teardown, and Windows worker startup.
