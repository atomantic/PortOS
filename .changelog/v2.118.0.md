# Release v2.118.0 - Cinematic Chameleon

Released: 2026-11-12

## Highlights

- **Blender films:** build music videos from Python-authored shots, render stills and previews, and watch the whole film live against the song.
- **Mood boards:** combine two boards into a new independent visual style, or complete a board's analysis, style and main image in one action.
- **Steadier pictures:** opening titles can slide into place and stay steady above film effects, while paused previews keep their picture.
- **More reliable lyric timing:** check repeated lines against the recording and apply suggested corrections without resetting verified timing on upgrade.
- **Clearer sync and usage:** Spotify shows failed background syncs, and usage estimates account for transcript-confirmed Fast and Ultrafast pricing.
- **Local production reliability:** image renders survive laptop sleep more gracefully, and supported local media runtimes receive reproducible installation and repair updates.

## Added

- Create Blender-film music videos with editable Python sources, song-aware shot timing, per-shot notes, render queues and preview, animatic or final cuts.
- Preview rendered Blender shots as a continuous film with live film looks, shot effects and title cards; completed renders appear without reloading.
- Change a film's declared settings, see which renders are out of date, fork its sources independently and clear regenerable frames to recover space.
- Combine mood boards into an independent style, visit their original sources, and complete missing visual analysis and a main image together.
- Slide opening title cards in from either side, set separate fade times and keep titles steady over film looks and held frames.
- Inspect film-look finishing phase timings to understand where rendering time goes.
- Restore Pixal3D CUDA installation with an isolated, locked runtime and an explicit lower-detail path when its optional native attention component cannot build.

## Changed

- Blender prioritizes queued stills and previews ahead of final renders while leaving running jobs intact.
- A previously successful Blender execution check can automatically run again after restart when the executable and saved configuration are unchanged.
- Codex and Grok usage estimates include Fast and Ultrafast premiums only when their transcripts identify that mode, while reconciliation preserves already-accounted usage.
- Refresh supported image, music, speech, rigging and local-model runtime dependencies, with hash-locked build tools and more predictable Install and Repair behavior.
- Improve retained-branch explanations, retry ownership and cleanup of merged work outside the usual managed checkout locations.

## Fixed

- Spotify retains failed sync details, shows when reconnection is needed and preserves its previous playback cursor for retry.
- Telegram notification forwarding and bridge failures are contained instead of escaping as unhandled errors.
- Repeated lyric lines are checked independently, nearby misplaced words are flagged, and separate cues cannot claim the same recognized occurrence.
- Film looks keep vignette corners dark and preserve authored color levels across final video and browser previews.
- Pausing, stepping and late-arriving decoded video frames keep document and film previews visible; empty surfaces clear correctly.
- Steady titles remain above film looks and frame holds in composed renders and excerpts, without appearing in cold opens or endings.
- Blender shot boundaries match the rendered frame grid, imported source changes invalidate affected renders, and older still thumbnails remain available.
- Local image renders hold a macOS sleep assertion, and the media watchdog excludes host suspension from runner-silence time.
- Suno song selection and draft preparation wait for observable search and editor readiness, preserving the manual caption fallback when needed.
- Mood-board composition preserves optical style, active edits and source ownership; comparison-sheet retries retain both generated halves.
- Data Manager preserves goal scorecard rules, Telegram check-in answers and protected counters during purges.
- PM2 status reads avoid starting daemons and handle long paths safely; development previews detect the primary checkout's HTTPS setup.

- Blender renders become out of date when the song or its analysis changes, refuse gaps at the song boundaries, and avoid mixing frames from different song contexts.
- Concurrent Blender sync and render requests preserve active output; same-shot stills finish serially, and cancellation during poster generation stays canceled.
- Converting Blender shots between frame rates keeps the intended motion instead of freezing the tail.
- Late mood-board job replies cannot replace newer results or disable another board, and Make complete explains when an existing analysis must finish first.
- Blender file refresh failures preserve unsaved drafts with a Retry, and render controls respect current locks, readiness and paused-frame positions.
- Cloning a film either finishes with its own song metadata or rolls back the new clone while preserving its source.
