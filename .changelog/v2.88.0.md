# Release v2.88.0 - Spotlight Otter

Released: 2026-10-06

## Highlights

- **A clearer path from song to release.** Music Video now walks through six steps, with a prominent player, concise approvals, and layouts that leave more room for the work on a phone.
- **Cover art with your own signature.** Design each song's cover, adjust its lettering directly, save artist styles, and use uploaded fonts or finished artwork.
- **Publishing with fewer surprises.** Prepare Spotify releases through DistroKid, work through one destination at a time, and see which platforms remain.
- **Smaller private sharing copies.** Download a verified music-video copy under 100 MB alongside the full-quality final.
- **More dependable backups.** Media, imported documents, recordings, and their saved records stay coordinated across supported backup and restore operations.
- **Scoped maintenance between your machines.** Granted peer maintenance gains durable recovery and protection against replay or uncertain execution.

## Added

- Music Video has dedicated Song, Look, Storyboard, Make, Final render, and Publish steps, each showing its status and next action.
- Code-drawn shots can be planned and rendered without asking for missing footage; mixed projects can identify code overlays on footage shots.
- Cover art supports image history, uploads, finished covers, adjustable title placement and typography, outline and stencil treatments, reusable artist styles, and uploaded fonts.
- Spotify via DistroKid joins the publishing destinations, with draft preparation for audio, cover art, genres, language, store profiles, songwriter details, and Apple Music credits; final agreements and submission remain with the operator.
- Private sharing downloads provide a reusable, verified MP4 below 100 MB without replacing the original final render.
- Projects with an existing final render can be marked as finished outside PortOS, avoiding requests for approvals that were never performed there.
- Video Gen can run fal lip sync with an uploaded voice clip, validating its duration before submission.
- Settings offers an opt-in, revocable Agent API key for terminal agents to call PortOS without handling the instance password.
- The local model catalog adds Gemma 4 E4B, Phi-4 Reasoning, and TranslateGemma 12B.

## Changed

- Storyboard, Make, and Final render place the player beside their approval controls on wide screens and keep drafts and proofs easy to find on smaller screens.
- Approval controls lead with the action, written notes are optional, and the animated proof is optional; footage production retains its pilot-proof check before bulk generation.
- Publishing names the next platform, counts the destinations left, and requires confirmation before preparing another post for a destination already used.
- Cover image generation follows the configured image provider, accepts creative direction, and prevents duplicate requests while preparation is in progress.
- The persistent mind's tools drawer groups grants and folds reference material for a shorter settings view.
- Deleted-record cleanup uses plain language and explains which sync settings can block it.

## Fixed

- Backup and restore coordination now covers more media completion, replacement, removal, import, archive, sprite, and recording operations, retaining recovery blockers when rollback is uncertain.
- Restoring selected individual files works with rsync 3, and restored peer execution records preserve ownership, revocation, and replay protection.
- Bundled composition assets resolve correctly in previews, and video seeking waits for the displayed frame before capture.
- Music-video navigation opens the newest project for a step-only link, and the latest draft for each range remains visible after edits.
- Cover generation recovers from interrupted requests, rotated phone photos crop upright, and the lettering switch follows changes made by restyling.
- DistroKid drafts avoid assuming new artist profiles, identify unanswered credits, check paid-extra selections, and label key controls for screen readers.
- Failed health, mail, vault, history, and project reads show a retry state instead of an empty list or misleading zero values.
- Branch reconciliation preserves worktrees outside its managed locations, while eligible follow-up agents can take over idle worktrees without deleting untracked files.
- Oversized review prompts report their actual size failure instead of a misleading credential error.
- An expanded credential-bootstrap section stays open when its initial results are empty.

## Removed

- The Activity by Hour dashboard widget is removed from built-in and saved layouts.

## Internal

- Browser suites now run explicitly in CI with their required dependencies, and several asynchronous, restore, and cross-platform fixtures have more reliable ownership and diagnostics.
- Updated proxy-addr and source-map-js dependencies are included.
