# Release v2.95.0 - Glassbox Glitch

Released: 2026-10-14

## Highlights

- **See exactly what the model gets:** Image Gen, Video Gen, music-video clips and stills, mood boards, FableLoom scenes, Music Designer, Enhance/Refine, and shot-card renders now show the exact final prompt and every addition PortOS makes, and long additions are shown whole instead of cut at 20,000 characters.
- **Music video effects:** VHS rewind / fast-forward glitches, VHS glitch and channel-change cut transitions, a beat toggle across cuts, glitches at any moment inside a shot, and still shots that fade, flash or glitch into their end frame, including camera-flash "develop" image-effect shots.
- **Shot-level control:** Per-shot start frame, clip speed and frame offset, a redesigned shot card with swap-from-unused-takes and start/end frame pairs, Previous / Next shot buttons, an Edit shot button in the preview player, and splitting at a picked time.
- **Lip-sync and audio:** Lip-sync shots can render a whole sung line, music-driven fal shots hear the song as an audio reference, and OmniHuman takes upload their frame and audio instead of failing.
- **Tighter host security:** Many sensitive actions (backup administration, outbound endpoints, mail, Beeper, social-bot publication, Jira and Datadog destinations, media process execution, camera and microphone access, and more) now require operator authority, and sprite, Digital Twin, GSD and app-icon paths are confined to their roots.
- **Providers and reliability:** One ordered provider fallback list under Models > Fallbacks, a refreshed local LLM catalog (Qwen3.6 27B, Magistral Small 24B, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning), a Reconcile button for local branches, provider-free Eidoverse riddle rounds, and many data-integrity fixes for Brain, journal, Apple Health and backups.

## Added

- Exact-prompt previews and labeled addition chips across image, video, music-video, mood-board, FableLoom and Music Designer renders.
- Music-video VHS rewind/fast-forward, glitch, channel-change and beat-toggle effects; still-to-end-frame fade, flash and glitch; image-effect shots.
- Per-shot start frame, clip speed and frame offset with matching live previews and document renders; optional title card at the end and a cutting-room-floor scene after the song.
- Generate new mood board images with a selectable prompt writer; sharper mood board video frames and a visual frame picker.
- Single ordered provider fallback list, a Reconcile button on the Git tab, and provider-free Eidoverse riddle rounds with explanations for intentionally stopped tasks.

## Changed

- Lip-sync review shows the shot's own words and drops checks nobody can act on; rejecting a take no longer marks a shot bad.
- Music-driven shots on fal and local LTX clips use the song audio and end frames more faithfully; fal video prompts stay within each model's length limit.
- Local LLM and Antigravity model catalogs refreshed.

## Fixed

- Sensitive actions across the app now require operator authority; path containment fixed for several import and read routes; scanner results are private and bounded.
- Persistent Mind acts on its wake instead of re-orienting every turn; Codex capacity and safety refusals are diagnosed and recovered.
- Brain, journal, Digital Twin, Apple Health and backup data are preserved across peer clock skew, reimports and finalization failures.
- Mobile layouts for agent cards, dashboard layout editor, run actions and the shot card.
- Job schedules advance after an execution failure instead of re-firing every second.
