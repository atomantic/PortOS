# Release v2.107.0 - Cinema Magpie

Released: 2026-10-29

## Highlights

- Deleting an image or video now travels safely between your machines: each delete waits for the machine that owns the media, shows as pending, and can be cancelled before it commits.
- Music videos gain a film credits ending, a Story pre-production step, festival submission packets, Topaz upscaling and a local cinema (DCP) export.
- Cast & Sets becomes interactive and story-driven, with per-look outfits, catalog suggestions and filters by story.
- You can see which machines hold a render and keep one copy with Make primary.
- Release publication and synchronization recovery is sturdier when agents are interrupted.

## Added

- Durable delete records for federated images and videos; Image Gen, Video Gen, Media History and the Video Downloader show pending and owner-unknown deletes with a cancel action.
- Machine ownership for media: existing videos and gallery images are backfilled with their origin machine, and a render's holders are listed with a Make primary action.
- Film credits ending (including a one-screen grid layout), a Story step, editable festival submission packets and an artist press and submission profile for music videos.
- Topaz video upscale workflow and local cinema DCP export for music videos.
- Film looks gain colour response, halation spread and a vintage lens; VHS and Polaroid looks are retuned.
- Several reference sheets can render at once; references can be rated good or bad and stepped through from the enlarged preview; Casting, Environments and Objects open in the standard media viewer and filter by story.
- Cast & Sets offers an update when linked Casting changes, and music-video cast import suggests existing catalog records.
- 4:3 large and small media generation presets and a one-step Edit action in the image lightbox.
- A roller-rink greybox starter with a regulation derby track.

## Changed

- Peer video history refreshes every five minutes from realtime-connected peers instead of every minute.
- Time Machine exclusion for PortOS directories is asked once.
- Quality audits weigh fix cost, and a new audit lane hunts disproportionate process machinery.

## Fixed

- Racing peer merges can no longer resurrect a deleted video or image row; restored images reach peers that hold their delete.
- Peer video deletes are accepted only from the owning paired peer, and machine-local fields stay out of served gallery sidecars and share buckets.
- Dead-peer video streams no longer stack up and flood the server log; peer-hosted media previews and host-mode renders stream correctly.
- Brain and memory peer sharing grants are enforced, and peer updates publish only after persistence succeeds.
- Blank music-video previews under a frozen composition host, and lyric playthrough previews for songs with many aligned words, now work.
- Cast & Sets cards no longer show stale reads, and age and look sheets edit from their own image.
- Release candidate, checkpoint and checkout recovery handle interrupted agents, locked checkouts and unverified files without losing work.
