# Release v2.64.0

Released: 2026-09-12

## Highlights

- **More dependable everyday navigation:** calendar, command-palette, tag, icon-picker, and entity-selection interactions now preserve focus, reveal the right content, and remain usable with keyboards and assistive technology.
- **Safer synchronization and backups:** peer sync now reports unavailable coverage and delivery obligations clearly, while backup failures and dangling-link compatibility are preserved for reliable recovery.
- **Richer Eidoverse and Mind continuity:** persistent Minds can choose durable names, retain their identity while thinking, wake on demand, and carry images and names across federated travel.
- **Cleaner media workflows:** video conditioning, hosted rendering, queue handoff, and render ownership now share more predictable preparation and cleanup behavior.
- **Better calendar and messaging operations:** agenda views start at today, month overflow opens every event, and Calendar and Messages sync surfaces report outcomes while releasing busy controls.
- **More precise provider and quality controls:** model defaults, provider overrides, Codex quota refreshes, and quality-category views expose the choices and evidence needed to operate a busy install.
- **More reliable story planning repairs:** arc verification can now correct existing countdown reminders without replacing the rest of the authored arc.
- **More complete foundation reviews:** story characters referenced by established aliases remain visible to planning checks, and alias changes invalidate cached judgments.

## Added

- App Quality exposes category-level highlights below the overall score.
- Persistent Minds support durable user-selected names, manual wake-up, and image attachments in Codex TUI conversations.
- Federated Eidoverse travel preserves names and selected identity across peers.
- Calendar month overflow opens all events in the overflow window, and agenda views begin with today's events.
- Arc resolve exposes persisted countdown metadata and applies sparse repairs to existing reminder IDs.

## Changed

- Command Palette selection, canonical tag suggestions, keyboard entity options, icon-picker dismissal, calendar drawers, and horizontal tab reveals preserve focus and keep the selected content visible.
- Backup and peer-sync flows retain actionable state when manifests, destinations, delivery coverage, or source links cannot be read.
- Video conditioning preparation, hosted video handoff, and cleanup share one clearer lifecycle, while process scans and provider/model controls avoid redundant work and expose explicit defaults.
- Persistent Mind, Calendar, Messages, and Eidoverse surfaces keep their live identity, sync progress, and responsive controls aligned across refreshes and federated updates.

## Fixed

- Failed model-abuse guard setup remains actionable after status refresh and remount instead of losing its diagnostic alert.
- CLI interruptions during server shutdown now retain their interruption result instead of being reported as successful completion.
- Backup failures remain persisted, dangling symlink backups retain compatibility, and incomplete full-sync coverage is reported instead of appearing complete.
- Pending tags survive chip removal, quality shortcuts avoid stale autopilot state, and explicit provider-default models remain selectable.
- Calendar and Messages sync outcomes no longer leave busy controls stuck, and standalone push updates and delayed sync starts settle correctly.
- Mind identity, chosen names, image attachments, and legacy identity prose remain consistent across saves, thinking states, and federated paths.
- Countdown metadata repairs preserve reminder IDs, omitted fields, and disabled-clock state while rejecting unsupported replacements.
- Foundation review now recognizes established character aliases in episode and arc references without making locked characters repair targets.
- PM2 log streams retain separate lines, Telegram polling stops after failed token validation, and annotation canvases use their default dimensions when omitted.
- The npm engine floor matches lockfile metadata support, and the Accelerate checkpoint vulnerability is mitigated while dependency alerts remain audited.
