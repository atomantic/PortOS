# Release v2.80.0 - Rhythm & Reels

Released: 2026-09-28

## Highlights

- **Music-video direction:** build a treatment, plan shots around lyrics and musical phrases, and choose the takes that tell your story.
- **Performance shots:** create source-audio lip-sync takes with fal.ai MiniMax H3, optionally guided by a separate vocal stem.
- **Composed edits:** combine footage, moving stills, title cards and timed typography while keeping the song continuous.
- **Draft review:** preview a short excerpt, leave timecoded notes and revise only flagged sections, with optional automatic review under explicit generation limits.
- **Launch videos:** publish MP4s with sound in landscape, vertical and square formats from one timeline, with style references and motion proofs.
- **Operator protection:** close additional host-control authorization gaps and keep video-provider credentials out of settings responses.

## Added

- Music Video treatments capture audience, destination, story arc, recurring motifs and shot direction, with a review of prompt changes before applying them.
- Timed lyrics, phrase annotations and pacing controls guide shot plans; long shots can split at lyric, phrase or beat boundaries.
- Scenes retain candidate frame and clip takes with explicit selection, rejection and review notes.
- Reusable visual specifications can pull universe references and travel in downloadable handoff bundles for external tools; imported clips return as scene takes.
- The Music Video board offers fal.ai alongside existing backends and explains which lanes support performance shots.
- Draft excerpts include cut-and-cue contact sheets and timecoded review notes; selective revisions resume without resubmitting completed generation.
- Opt-in automatic draft review uses chosen models and explicit review and generation limits, and projects can mix an optional sound-design bed beneath the song.
- Launch videos gain motion styles, proof-and-critique rounds, image or video style references, optional motion blur and beat-aligned timing.
- Suno exports can be imported manually into the music library with lyrics, style and source information.
- Media prompt examinations can be saved and reused.
- A development-environment audit measures temporary files and other host effects left by development commands.

## Changed

- Media uploads stream without file-size caps while retaining decoded-image safety checks.
- Launch-video formats are grouped into one take with a format switcher and individual downloads.
- Long-track beat and audio analysis bounds decoded audio to five minutes to limit memory use.
- Music-video and music-track synchronization versions advance to protect new timing, take-selection and provenance fields; update peers before syncing these records.

## Fixed

- Concurrent database recovery requests reuse the correct replacement worker instead of attempting a stale launch.

- Cloning a project during a draft render leaves the source render's files untouched.
- Planned shot durations remain accurate when sparse beats require off-beat cuts.

- Cancelling media generation is honored before provider dispatch; paused and cancelled draft reviews stop handing out work and preserve their checkpoints.
- Restart recovery preserves renders owned by another peer and cleans up interrupted local video outputs.
- Performance takes cannot silently loop or render against a changed song or timing, and imported non-MP4 clips play using their stored format.
- Concurrent treatment, lyric, pacing and take-selection saves preserve newer edits; failed saves keep typed notes available.
- Typography direction stays out of generated image and video prompts, and long composed text stays inside the safe area.
- Music renders remain alive during inference, and repeated launch-video delivery is refused before an unnecessary capture.
- Additional protections cover Brain Notes vault selection and file containment, agent instructions and tool prompts, GitHub mutations, Quota Burn, shell images and Image Gen setup interpreters.
- Ask and AI app detection enforce tool-free execution or require operator authority for agent-capable providers; trailing slashes no longer bypass host-control checks.
- fal.ai and Reactor credentials remain write-only in settings responses.
- Database setup preserves the selected database when Docker is unavailable.
- Scheduled task titles no longer truncate on mobile, and claim worktrees receive dependency links for running checks.
- Test runs clean up temporary resources more reliably and avoid unintended real CLI probes.
