# Release v2.108.0 - Sketchbook Heron

Released: 2026-10-31

## Highlights

- Music videos get a full Storyboard step: draw panels from each shot, sketch right on a frame, pick framing, camera angle, move, lens and eyeline, and time panels to the song with shots kept one-to-one with them.
- Cold opens: music videos can now start with shots before the song begins.
- Casting grows into a design studio: design a cast member through rounds of face variations, fork one from picked seed images, redesign from its current canon, and revise a look from Cast & Sets.
- Photo sequences can play inside a still shot and overlap as a layered photographic collage, alongside new analog film motion, stepped and beat-graphic production styles.
- The media library shows where each image and clip is used and guards deleting media that is still in use; each media provider gets its own render queue lane.
- Phones and tablets are much easier to use: touch-sized controls, drag-to-reorder that works by touch, modal footers that stay on screen in iOS Safari, and no more pages scrolling sideways.
- Pages that fail to load now say so and offer Retry instead of showing empty or default data, and a failed read can no longer save defaults over your settings.
- Privacy and security tightening: spoken, typed and searched text, blood pressure readings and calendar names stay out of logs, and several host-control and peer checks are stricter.
- Deep quality audits run as a single long agent per check, and audits file what they find outside their own category instead of leaving notes.

## Added

- Storyboard step for music videos with a panel grid, side-panel editing, Redraw all, drawing on a frame with a correction note, and inserting panels before or after the selected one.
- Storyboard panel pickers for framing (including drawn shot diagrams), camera angle, camera move, lens, focus, eyeline, subject facing and composition, plus a classic greybox sketch style.
- Storyboard panels take song sections from the lyric sheet, play their beats before the song, and stay timed to it.
- Cold-open shots before the song in music videos.
- Casting: design rounds of face variations, fork a cast member from picked seed images or save a redesign as a fork, redesign from current canon, pick blend faces from a mood board, and add forked members to their universe's cast.
- Music-video frames made from an older Casting look are marked, and plates are checked against each other before video steps.
- Photo sequences within a still shot, layered photo collages, tunable and repeating analog film motion with in-shot film events, and stepped and beat-graphic production mediums.
- Vertical lyrics and titles stay clear of social-player overlay zones.
- Image Edit accepts reference images beside the image being edited.
- Media usage view showing where each image and clip is used, with a guard on deleting used media.
- A separate render queue lane for every media provider.
- Delete a consented voice-clone recording, and erase imported health measurements and lab results.
- Same-host monitors can read mind and Eidoverse status without signing in.
- Antigravity's model catalog includes Gemini 4 Argon.

## Changed

- Deep quality audits are one agent per check that may run for hours, and every audit files verified findings outside its category under the owning audit instead of listing them as recommendations.
- The music video Plan step is renamed Storyboard, and shots are planned before the Look.
- Storyboard panel editors drop timing, dialogue and camera-move fields that belong to the shot.
- Shot and storyboard side editors open wider on desktop.
- CLI code reviewers get the remote timeout budget, and Time Machine no longer asks for sudo on every update for paths already excluded.
- Merged-PR checkouts are retired automatically, including release candidates, without being held for leftover files.

## Fixed

- Many pages (Chief of Staff transcript, briefing, learning and providers; app tabs; MeatSpace and POST; Messages; Google OAuth; GitHub automation; storyboards; Digital Twin; the Brain graph and more) now show load failures with Retry instead of empty or misleading content, and late answers no longer overwrite newer ones.
- Failed MeatSpace and Messages reads can no longer save defaults over your profile, quick-add buttons or stored settings.
- Touch and phone layout fixes across checkbox rows, POST drills, image references, page headers, the Media catalog, Daily Log, copy-id and Show more controls, hover-revealed row actions and the timeline Remove button.
- Gallery tiles load small WebP thumbnails on phones.
- Pinterest mood-board imports of share links and single Pins now show their image.
- Image Gen no longer fails validation when a style preset carries a visual profile.
- Brain deletions reach every peer, newer Digital Twin edits and taste answers sync without being replaced, and peer replicas are adopted whenever this machine advances them.
- Restart recovery no longer runs a peer's Creative Director project, and the CI notification agent stays on runs the operator triggered.
- Host and OAuth checks treat every 127.x address as loopback, refuse cross-site host-control requests without an Origin header, and refuse 0.0.0.0 as a manually added peer.
- Voice calls re-attach after a socket reconnect and release the mic when an attach is refused.
- A failed self-update rolls the checkout back, CoS stops admitting agents during shutdown, and new PortOS-stack apps pass CI on their first push.
- The quota-burn plan no longer re-sends a finished Deep audit.
