# Release v2.93.0 - Storyboard Sentinel

Released: 2026-10-11

## Highlights

- **Music videos plan and film with the song itself.** A new song is analyzed and its lyric timing aligned automatically (re-aligned on the isolated vocal), the Suno style and excluded styles inform the design, lyrics show over the storyboard animatic, and videos open with an MTV-style title card. Music-driven performance shots run on fal audio-to-video models, and supplied song audio is padded past the end instead of crashing renders.
- **Autopilot goes further and stops where you want.** Autopilot can start a new video on an existing project's song, stop after the stills, have the orchestrator check them, and keep a reused song's media choices. Approval and stills checkpoints no longer park on stale pilots, proofs or storyboards, or clear work after a cancelled run.
- **Cast & Sets and the film look.** Character sheets are conditioned on the director's references, plain-text feedback applies to a whole sheet or one image, mood board people stay out of plates and frames, and a tunable film look applies to previews, renders and gallery stills.
- **More actions require operator authority.** Local music generation, video upscale and stitch, 3D generation, voice fine-tuning, LoRA training, Importer/Brain/analytics AI, Calendar MCP, cover design, promotion plans, POST drills, voice-config edits and automatic repository clone are refused for callers without operator authority; Moltbook posts and challenges are screened before tool-capable providers.
- **Sturdier local database and sync.** The local database start waits for TCP and the base schema, repairs a stale postmaster with the pinned image, and the browser health server binds to loopback only. Brain memory links survive interrupted syncs and digest/review/journal catch-up covers every day; catalog re-imports preserve identity and media.
- **Polish.** Outlook sync honors saved row selectors and finds the real scroll container, Calendar and Messages show Google OAuth setup progress, Importer and Deck cards stay readable at extreme zoom, and Rapid Reader puts text and actions first.

## Added

- Music-driven performance shots on fal audio-to-video models; MTV-style opening title card.
- Automatic song analysis and lyric alignment; lyrics over the storyboard animatic.
- Autopilot: stop after stills, orchestrator check of stills, new video on an existing song.
- Tunable film look; text-to-image, image-to-image and video send buttons on mood board cards.

## Changed

- Operator-authority checks on local generation, training, Importer/Brain/analytics AI, Calendar MCP and related routes.
- Image generation no longer applies a default negative prompt; fal LTX-2.3 dropped in favor of OmniHuman for music-driven video.
- Codex audit agents use Codex Security.

## Fixed

- Padded song audio keeps the last shot and LTX audio-to-video renders from crashing; draft renders no longer cut away to a stale lip-sync shot.
- Stills checkpoints record approval only when every still was seen; cancelled runs no longer clear stills.
- Fixture cleanup, Stacker News interrupted actions, partial settings saves and Brain link healing.
- Release tags stay pinned to the verified commit when archiving the changelog.
