# Release v2.112.0 - Director Dragonfly

Released: 2026-11-06

## Highlights

- Plan a music video in a shot sheet with editable actions, cameras, locations, time of day, and a timeline map that filters the shots beside it.
- Cast & Sets follows live catalog links, offers linked character sheets and prop images in reference pickers, and rebuilds its visual guide from the current catalog.
- Persistent Minds can review their own memories, retain a self-written continuation note, and recall memories relevant to your current message.
- Codex usage accounting handles large session histories more reliably and offers bounded, read-only previews that explain coverage gaps without rewriting past totals.
- Gemini video generation gains a browser-driven option using the signed-in PortOS Browser.
- More screens preserve existing work and offer Retry when a read fails, and mobile controls are easier to tap.

## Added

- Use the music-video shot sheet to review lyrics, timing, camera ownership, framing, location, light, cast, action, emotion, notes, and the actual prompts without rendering.
- Check shots manually, filter incomplete or unchecked rows, set columns across listed shots, and spot missing camera assignments or outdated selected frames.
- Request AI proposals to fill empty sheet columns or turn checked rows into prompts, inspect the exact request, and choose which edits to apply.
- Follow location, camera, and time-of-day tracks on the video map; selecting a stretch filters both the sheet and scene board through a shareable URL.
- Set each camera to photos or video and explicitly match its shots without starting renders.
- Save an unlinked music-video subject to the catalog directly from Manage catalog links, or fork a catalog identity with editable descriptions.
- Rebuild the catalog-linked Cast & Sets guide with current images, protagonists, alternates, objects, and shot times.
- Generate video through Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash in the signed-in browser, with its own progress display and reference uploads; accepting upload terms remains an operator action.
- Let Persistent Minds review owned memories, preserve linked lessons, optionally reflect on fading memories, and carry their own continuation note across compaction.
- Preview bounded native Codex usage history and coverage without changing the accounting ledger.

## Changed

- Music-video shot reference pickers offer linked portraits, sheets, guides, and suitable references for characters, sets, and props in a larger gallery.
- Switching a music video to catalog links carries matching saved shot references forward and names picks that still need attention.
- Directors can render individual shot stills and clips while review advice remains visible; production runs retain their approval and defect checks.
- Shot split, merge, start, end, and duration controls live together, and timing changes also update a linked storyboard when supported.
- Import a shot clip directly from a video file, jump to the start of a preview, and read shot names and actions that stay aligned after a recut.
- Persistent Mind message recall uses the current message to search its own active memories locally, within the existing context budget.
- Usage diagnostics distinguish provider and instance coverage, unpriced tokens, and unknown historical overlap, with explicit recovery limits.
- Homebrew-managed harnesses and Ollama compare against versions Homebrew can actually install and explain releases still waiting on packaging.

## Fixed

- Preserve director edits when applying AI shot-sheet proposals, retain the sheet through replanning, and keep every successfully saved row visible during batch edits.
- Keep manual split choices inside valid fractional shot boundaries and refuse linked timing edits that a storyboard would silently shorten.
- Refuse missing or outdated catalog references before a paid render dispatch, keep reference image identities distinct, and respect the selected cast alternate.
- Keep catalog-linked descriptions current, distinguish removing a Look reference from unlinking its catalog image, and allow repeated catalog merges with valid reverse recovery.
- Reject a catalog guide rebuild if its catalog changes during image encoding and use current project timing when assembling the saved guide.
- Explain which images remain after film finishing fails and link blocked proof deletion to replacement controls.
- Contain Gemini browser upload failures within their jobs, stop on visible generation errors, and avoid offering unsupported negative-prompt or duration controls.
- Preserve Codex counters across nearby restarts, deduplicate moved or compressed session sources, skip irrelevant large transcript lines, and isolate incomplete Codex scans from other providers.
- Roll back rejected usage-accounting transactions and retain atomic media-accounting repairs.
- Build peer integrity manifests for all supported record kinds and handle timestamp ordering, tied timestamps, and older-peer capabilities explicitly.
- Preserve Eidoverse travel destinations when a probe fails and page accepted animation assets with stable cursors.
- Show read failures and Retry on Digital Twin, agent lists, Loops, process snapshots, and Brain scan reports while retaining usable data and forms during refresh errors.
- Enlarge touch targets across navigation, confirmations, Catalog, Brain, inbox, peers, agents, scheduling, and Chief of Staff insights.
- Keep historical message subjects out of triage requests and redact senders from correction logs.
- Restart UI-triggered Node updates on the newly selected interpreter and distinguish a stale service from a release still waiting on Homebrew.
