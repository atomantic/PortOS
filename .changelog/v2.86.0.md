# Release v2.86.0 - Steady Spotlight

Released: 2026-10-04

## Highlights

- **Music Video:** clearer production status, evidence beside review decisions, and recovery after reloads make long creative runs easier to follow.
- **Mobile access:** previews wait until opened on phones, while larger touch targets and better keyboard focus make everyday controls easier to use.
- **Beeper:** search conversations, save named views, snooze threads into Later, and open a new direct conversation without sending a message.
- **Backup and restore:** more media changes coordinate with snapshots, and database restores preserve the destination's authority and installed extensions.
- **Writing tools:** Story Builder reconnects to active work, and Reverse Outline and Continuity Bible recover when progress streams end unexpectedly.
- **AI diagnostics:** test provider vision from provider cards and see Digital Twin results as each model finishes.

## Added

- Music Video projects can be renamed, use local music studio options, and edit individual lyric word timings.
- Beeper read receipts are available through an explicit opt-in consent step.
- Approved Outlook and Teams drafts can be delivered through the signed-in browser tab; unsupported drafts offer a copy action.
- Eidoverse explains how local districts receive attention.
- Autofixer exposes staged patches and promotion controls.

## Changed

- Music Video keeps a playable preview available across stages, preserves folded panels and the selected preview, and distinguishes current review requirements from stale history.
- Authenticated browser and agent sessions can review creative work using evidence tied to the selected revision, without repeated password entry; public publication remains a human action.
- Autonomous setup offers local image and video model choices and clearer immediate progress.
- Creative Director, Pipeline, and other detail pages use more consistent headers and status displays.
- Repeated Beeper activity updates avoid unnecessary writes.
- Unavailable or unfinished controls no longer imply working social ingestion, forwarding templates, or unsupported unread-only synchronization.

## Fixed

- Music Video reconnects to final renders and publishing-kit builds after reloads, exposes interrupted work, and handles start, resume, and cancellation under one job owner.
- Phone composition previews avoid loading every scene while collapsed, reducing memory pressure; resizing to desktop also loads the preview.
- Music Video dependency repair no longer crashes while displaying an informational notification, and code-only projects accept rendered storyboard frames and animatics as review evidence.
- Manuscript fixes and undo preserve newer edits, keep results attached to the right series, and prevent another mutation while a retained draft is unsaved.
- LoRA caption batches and creative imports retain progress across reloads so work can be recovered instead of duplicated.
- Brain readers, digests, graph views, Daily Log editing, and dashboard threads report failed reads and support recovery without discarding useful state.
- Notifications, calendar events, navigation controls, popovers, and dialogs improve touch access, keyboard focus, and small-screen layout.
- Backup snapshots coordinate with music takes, gallery changes, LoRA datasets, voice profiles, and music-video asset publication; broader asset-owner coverage remains ongoing.
- Database restore preserves machine-local authority, handles provisioned extensions and newer-client dumps, and retains maintenance recovery until journal changes are durable.
- Docker database imports retain their SQL input instead of silently completing without importing it.
- Teams draft delivery requires the exact intended recipient address when selecting a conversation.
- Voice tool sessions revalidate correctly, and agent-backed Music Video and Pipeline operations preserve operator authority.
- Agent completion files survive interrupted writes, active spawns avoid premature cleanup, and worktree recovery preserves ownership and reports failed removals.
- Local speech setup selects the correct published platform archive, and media installation only reports success after verified completion.

## Upgrade notes

- Music Video proof approval requires recorded inspection evidence; old automatic proof waivers no longer satisfy readiness. Existing valid art and playback approvals remain valid.
- Creative approvals use an authenticated browser or agent session. Agent-backed Music Video and Pipeline actions enforce operator authority while retaining authorized local and delegated-agent workflows.

## Internal

- Expanded route-validation, database-contract, workflow-recovery, and accessibility regression coverage.
- Updated PostgreSQL, icon, test, and lint dependencies already merged into development.
- Refreshed the published quality snapshot from local numeric assessments.
