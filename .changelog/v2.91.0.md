# Release v2.91.0 - Steady Splicer

Released: 2026-10-09

## Highlights

- **Reshape a music video mid-project.** Revise a project's song from the Song step (instrumentals are re-timed instead of failing), and merge a Board scene with the next one as the inverse of split, with guards against merges that would produce an impossible timeline.
- **Toon worlds get real ink.** Shared toon materials, shells and visible ink outlines, with palette bands chosen from continuous lighting and the ink pass skipped on frames that do not need it.
- **Safer host-powered authoring.** Digital Twin AI actions, Story Builder generation, Universe Builder agents and FableLoom authoring now require operator authority.
- **Mail you can trust.** Gmail replies keep their original message and thread identity, a lost send acknowledgement leaves delivery "unknown" rather than guessing, Quick Reply drafts carry the right sender and subject, and failed reads no longer drop Messages drafts or triage rules.
- **Videos save on phones.** Video downloads work reliably on mobile Safari, and several settings, catalog and media screens now fit short phone screens.
- **Database and backup clarity.** Docker and CLI database tooling follows the selected endpoint and exported mode, degraded backups show as failures in Eidoverse health, and Apple Health archive and backup behavior is disclosed up front.
- **Sturdier agents and CI.** Branch reconciliation handles detached and superseded worktrees, merge admission is held only around the final verify-and-merge, and browser-based tests clean up stalled Chromium processes.

## Added

- Revising a music video's song mid-project from the Song step.
- Merging a Board scene with the next one.
- Shared toon-world materials, shells and visible ink.
- Operator-authority checks on Digital Twin AI actions, Story Builder generation, Universe Builder agents and FableLoom authoring.

## Changed

- Merge admission is held only around the final verify-and-merge; the quality snapshot publisher defers while a claim holds it.
- Ready agent branches merge without chasing a moving main.
- Reviewer refusals are explained, and a pinned Antigravity review wrapped in a markdown fence is accepted.

## Fixed

- Video downloads on mobile Safari; prepared files are released when choosing a browser download.
- Gmail reply identity, delivery-unknown after a lost acknowledgement, Quick Reply drafts, serialized triage corrections, and drafts kept after failed reads.
- Music video polish: excerpt typography matches the full video, hooks cut at the next cue and stay inside their text zone, lyric fades finish before the next caption, canceled renders are preserved, end frames are honored in fal video generation, and draft timestamps use local time.
- Code Animation repairs stop on cancel or deadline and release their disk charges; accepted output survives history failures.
- Health imports and recovery archives are preserved, including archives that share a timestamp.
- Peer sync: media index repairs and incomplete task merges retry, and share buckets keep source version clocks.
- Manuscript imports admit one commit at a time per series and resume safely.
- Database credentials, bootstrap and maintenance respect the selected endpoint and `PGMODE`.
- Phone and short-screen layouts for LoRA, media settings, the catalog editor and Story Builder; long Story Builder titles wrap.
- Windows Codex image CLI launches and image CLI job settlement.
- Branch reconciliation removes or reports detached worktrees and reaps verified-superseded branches.
- Many timing-sensitive tests and CI jobs were made deterministic.
