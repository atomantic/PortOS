# Release v2.97.0 - Sturdy Reel

Released: 2026-10-17

## Highlights

- **Faster, steadier music-video rendering:** Shots now render four at a time with retries, the shots rendered so far play while the rest finish, and each film look is kept so you can swap back. The film look is drawn on the GPU whenever it matches the CPU filter, with defocus and color fringes matched to the CPU result.
- **Shot editing:** Per-shot film look tuning with saved looks and 16:9 players, a Lock all button on the Shots page, Merge over previous on the shot card, effect chips on each Shots board card, and shots that are not locked yet listed under Needs attention. Render refusals offer a one-click render of a new clip.
- **Playback correctness:** Intercut never plays a shot's tail just before that shot starts, beat toggles play the incoming shot, repeated frames and paused-footage colors are fixed, and storyboard stills animate and save the displayed frame.
- **Cancel and restart safety:** Canceling an autonomous music video or a local image/video render stops work that is still preparing, a re-render right after a cancel no longer joins the cancelled one, and a restart clears a finished render's stale error. Shot files produced during a final render are no longer pruned.
- **Sync and sharing:** Peers and share buckets converge after a restart, undelivered tombstones survive manual sweeps, a running share export can no longer resurrect an unshared or deleted record, and schema-gap updates are serialized. Sharing pages show load failures instead of empty states.
- **Reliability under failure:** A failed launch can no longer crash the CoS Runner or wedge the GPU lane, held autonomous jobs stop re-firing every second, zombie-swept agents emit completion, and OpenCode startup problems fail fast. Gmail, Calendar, OAuth, PM2, `gh` and provider CLI probes are now time-bounded, and partial calendar syncs recover.
- **Brain and CoS:** Brain digest and weekly review are opt-in, Brain memory resyncs persist, and a capture that fails to file moves to needs review. CoS shows a status pill on the avatar, deleted tasks leave the Tasks tab immediately, and update refuses an unsupported Node runtime before touching the checkout.
- **Models and creative tools:** Gemini 4 Argon pricing and score in model comparison, a separate Antigravity Argon quota on the usage page, A/B YouTube thumbnail sets, complete-frame previews for new Canvas and Three.js documents, and a Codex image model fix.

## Added

- Four-way parallel shot rendering with retries and playback of finished shots.
- Per-shot film look tuning, saved looks, Lock all, Merge over previous, effect chips.
- A/B thumbnail set for YouTube Test & Compare; Gemini 4 Argon pricing and quota display.
- Quality audit of measured CI efficiency and stage placement.

## Changed

- Brain digest and weekly review are opt-in and arm after listening starts.
- Many routes return standard error envelopes with store paths redacted, and validate query parameters.
- Model weight downloads require POST; agent API calls over HTTPS use the local HTTP mirror.

## Fixed

- Render, cancel, restart, lyric-alignment and frame-sync defects in music-video production.
- Sync, sharing and Brain persistence races; calendar and Gmail partial failures.
- Numerous time bounds on external commands and many flaky or slow tests.
