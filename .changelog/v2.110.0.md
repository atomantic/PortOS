# Release v2.110.0 - Silver Screen Swift

Released: 2026-11-04

## Highlights

- Music Video color grading is dramatically faster, with matching looks across full exports, excerpts, and wipes.
- Render Environment cameras in Blender, dress scenes with editable AI material and lighting proposals, and export camera fly-throughs.
- Cut shots on sung words, preview a new pacing plan, and add shape, pattern, and moving wipes without shifting the song's cuts.
- Keep recurring faces and props consistent with face sheets, prop sheets, model-aware clip references, and reusable cameos.
- Find managed components and their updates together on the new Settings > Software page.
- Usage combines participating machines while avoiding duplicate subscription quota cards.

## Added

- Environment Photoreal renders support EEVEE previews and Cycles finals, saved camera selection, downloadable Blender scenes, and reusable photo plates.
- Environment looks can propose materials, lights, haze, exposure, and world lighting; inspect the full prompt and apply the editable preview when ready.
- Environment fly-throughs move through selected cameras and save a video in Media History.
- Music Video cameos keep a reusable still, variants, research photos, and on-screen labels for recurring subjects without requiring catalog records.
- Research and seed finds real reference photos for a shot with a chosen web-capable agent and keeps source links beside the photos.
- Shot transitions include iris, star, heart, diamond, blinds, checkerboard, clock, push, and other wipes, with a Vary wipes action for multiple shots.
- Board shots can anchor their cuts to lyric words and follow later vocal alignment, including Board-backed composition documents.
- Board pacing previews can split and re-cut shots around lyric starts, downbeats, and beats before applying the result.
- Cast & Sets supports face-angle sheets for every cast member, recurring prop sheets, image edits, selection from prior takes, and repeat vision inspection.
- Clip reference controls show which images the selected renderer receives and its image limit, with support for additional Grok, local, and hosted reference models.
- Settings > Software groups managed runtimes, harnesses, browser tools, and media components, with Blender installation and updates on macOS.
- Media cards and shot takes offer Copy ID and Copy URL, including a selectable fallback when clipboard access is unavailable.
- Local generation adds FLUX.3 Klein MLX choices and an executable Pika H3 Flex speed profile; the local language-model catalog adds GPT-OSS 120B and LFM3 models.
- An offline greybox export tool produces reproducible blocking sequences, camera clips, and reference frames.

## Changed

- Color grading uses lookup tables instead of repeated per-pixel expressions; the measured 1080p grading pass is about 20–37 times faster on the benchmark footage.
- Music Video planning defaults to shots no longer than three seconds when no project pacing is set.
- Face, set, prop, and research references follow the effective renderer's capacity and are labeled in the composed prompt.
- Codex can inspect generated images in a read-only session and is the default image reviewer when enabled; explicit reviewer choices remain respected.
- Local vision findings receive an independent second look before driving a defect decision, reducing false automatic retakes.
- Blender 5.2 works with Code Animation and rigging while existing Blender 4.2 projects retain compatibility.
- Manual updates remain available while automatic updates wait for active work, with explicit busy warnings and recovery for stale waiting supervisors.
- Usage respects peer participation and billing selections, selects one current quota view per provider, and preserves incomplete or failed readings visibly.
- Icon controls retain dense desktop layouts while meeting independent width and height touch targets on coarse-pointer devices.
- Agent cards show their launch origin, and split GitHub epics link their slices as native sub-issues.

## Fixed

- Wipes preserve each picture's own color grade, selected effects, and timing across preview, export, and excerpts; long edits no longer exceed filter-expression nesting limits.
- Board-backed document projects can merge and reorder shots, and excerpt render slots are released before completion is announced.
- Replacing a linked song offers re-timing or keeping the existing timing, and stale vocal stems explain how to regenerate them.
- Cast & Sets preserves authored expressions, intentional scene details, manual catalog links, and chosen images through re-approval, regeneration, and failed reads.
- Model-aware reference delivery preserves selected cast inputs through local, hosted, resumed, and peer-routed video workflows.
- Research agents can work from their scratch directory and stop with a useful partial result instead of searching until timeout.
- Photoreal numeric fields can be typed normally, and clearing every camera keeps the selection empty instead of selecting them all again.
- Conversation privacy controls erase the selected Signal, Gmail, Beeper, WhatsApp, or Discord conversation without conflating unrelated threads; genetic-data deletion also removes derived identity snapshots.
- Ask, voice startup, meeting capture, downloads, provider settings, lettering, and message delivery preserve newer state through overlapping reads or navigation.
- Managed-app discovery and port edits handle exported configuration, environment keys, aliases, and platform-specific listeners more reliably.
- Release recovery retains its frozen candidate, verifies process settlement, and can retire an abandoned legacy candidate without resurrecting it.
- Agent investigations recognize earlier merged work and overturned findings instead of repeatedly redispatching completed tasks.
- Shared quality history retains measurements from independently updated installations.
